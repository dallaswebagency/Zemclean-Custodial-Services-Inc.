    Frequently Asked Questions

    Straight answers about how we work, where we work and how to get started.

    Still have a question? Call or text us and we will get back to you. 780-289-4650 Send a text
    What types of commercial properties do you clean?
    We clean medical offices and clinics, apartments and condos, car dealerships, warehouses, service bays, construction sites, offices and commercial buildings across Edmonton and nearby communities. We also provide periodic cleanup services.
    Do you offer customized janitorial cleaning schedules?
    Yes. Every facility is different, so we build the cleaning schedule and scope around your building, your operations, and your needs.
    Can cleaning services be scheduled outside regular business hours?
    Yes. Night-time cleaning is available so your team arrives to a clean workplace without disruption to the working day.
    How do you maintain consistent cleaning quality?
    Through trained staff, supervision, and regular quality checks, with adjustments made as your facility's needs change.
    Are your cleaning staff professionally trained?
    Yes. Our team is trained in cleaning procedures, equipment handling, the responsible use of cleaning products, and workplace safety.
    Which areas around Edmonton do you serve?
    We serve Edmonton, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan.
    How can I request a commercial cleaning quote?
    Use the quote form on this page or call 780-289-4650. Tell us about your facility and we will come back with a customized cleaning proposal.
