About this service

Electrostatic Disinfection in Edmonton.

Electrostatic spraying coats surfaces evenly, including hard-to-reach areas, and covers large spaces quickly.

It is an effective way to disinfect shared spaces, and it can be booked once or added to your regular cleaning.

What is included

  • Electrostatic spray application
  • High-touch surfaces
  • Washrooms and shared areas
  • Offices, clinics and waiting rooms
  • One-time or recurring service
  • Planned around your hours

What does Electrostatic Disinfection include?
Electrostatic spray application, high-touch surfaces, washrooms and shared areas, offices, clinics and waiting rooms, one-time or recurring service, and more depending on your facility. We confirm the full scope in your quote.
What is electrostatic disinfection?
A spray method that gives surfaces an even coating of disinfectant, including hard-to-reach areas.
Can it be added to regular cleaning?
Yes, or booked as a one-time service.
