A Husband & Wife Team

We are a husband and wife team, who discovered the love for clean spaces almost 23 years ago. We put our passions into developing our business in 2002.

Over the last 21 years Zemclean has grown from being a small ma and pa type business to a fully accredited and professional team of supervisors, managers and reliable staff; 80% of our staff has been with us for over 18 years now.

At Zemclean we take great pride in continually educating ourselves to match the needs of our competitive industry. From new and effective chemicals to personal health and safety we never shy away from investing in our company. We take extra care when choosing chemicals and products keeping environment and health in mind. With changing times and industry challenges, we are adaptable and ready at a moment's notice.
